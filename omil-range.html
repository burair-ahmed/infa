Omil Range

Under our brand Omil, we offer a diverse variety of nutrition products which provide complete & balanced nutrition for different stages of life, be it the early days of your child's life, or for women during pregnancy & lactation. Omil Formula product offerings include Omil S1-Infant Formula, Omil S2-Follow Up Formula, Omil Grow-Growing Up Formula, Omil LF-Lactose Free Formula & Omil Mama-Nutritional Supplement for Pregnant & Lactating Mothers.

Omil - 1

Infant Formula recommended for infants from 0-6 months of age

Omil S1 is enriched with all the vital nutrients needed to fuel up the amazing rate of growth & development of your baby from early stage of life.

  • Contains high quality and optimum protein level for the growth and development of infant with 60:40 Whey Casein ratio to mimic the amino acid profile of breast milk and to assure better absorption and digestion of protein
  • Omil S1 provide adequate level of Carbohydrates to fulfill energy requirements of infants
  • Enriched with Omega-3 (Alpha Linolenic Acid) & Omega-6 (Linoleic Acid) fatty acids which are the precursors of DHA & ARA for proper brain and visual development in babies
  • Contains adequate Zinc level to help reduce the incidence of diarrhea
  • Contains Calcium, Vitamin D & Vitamin K for bone mineralization
  • Enriched with Choline to help in brain and memory development of infant
  • Optimum levels of Vitamins and Minerals for improved immunity outcomes

Omil - 2

Follow-Up Formula recommended for infants from 6-12 months of age

Omil S2 is a complete, nutritionally balanced follow-up formula designed to fulfil the nutritional needs of a growing baby as he starts solid food.

  • Contains high quality and optimum Protein level for healthy growth and development
  • Enriched with Omega-3 (Alpha Linolenic Acid) & Omega-6 (Linoleic Acid) fatty acids which are the precursors of DHA & ARA for proper brain and visual development in infants
  • Contain essential Vitamins & Minerals to help improve immunity outcomes
  • Contains adequate level of Iron which contributes to normal cognitive development and help to prevent iron deficiency anemia in babies
  • Enriched with adequate level of Vitamin A for proper maintenance of normal vision in infants

Omil Grow

Growing-Up Formula recommended for toddlers from 1 to 3 years of age

Omil Grow provides levels of protein and certain nutrients, vitamins and minerals which are required to fulfill your child's growing-up nutritional needs during the magical first 3 years of life.

  • Contain good quality and optimum Protein level for healthy growth
  • Enriched with Omega-3 (Alpha Linolenic Acid) & Omega-6 (Linoleic Acid) fatty acids for brain & visual development
  • Contain a unique blend of four vegetable oils for easier fat absorption
  • Contains Calcium, Vitamin D & Vitamin K for bone mineralization
  • Enriched with Choline to help in brain and memory development
  • Enriched with adequate Zinc level to help reduce the incidence of diarrhea
  • Contain optimum levels of Vitamins and Minerals for improved immunity outcomes
  • Omil Grow is available in delicious Vanilla flavor which makes the product palatable for toddlers

Omil - LF

Lactose Free Infant Formula recommended from 0 to 12 months of age for infants with lactose intolerance

Omil LF formula contains a unique formulation for the dietary management of fussiness, gas, diarrhea and abdominal pain due to lactose intolerance in infants.

  • It contain Corn Syrup Solids as lactose substitute or major Carbohydrate source. CSS are easy to digest & provide effective control of diarrhea due to lactose intolerance
  • As it contains no lactose, it makes a proper nutrition for infants who suffer from diarrhea, fussiness, gas or abdominal pain when drinking general infant formulas
  • Contain Nucleotides to promote development, growth & maturation of GI tract & to help reduce the incidence & severity of diarrhea
  • Enriched with Essential Fatty Acids, Linoleic Acid & α-Linolenic Acid which are critical for growth & development, LA & ALA are precursors of ARA & DHA to support brain & visual development
  • Provide infants with the daily requirements of Protein, Vitamins & Minerals to promote healthy growth

Omil Mama

Omil Mama is a complete nutritional supplement for pre-pregnancy, pregnancy and lactating mothers

  • Omil Mama is a cow milk based low fat milk powder scientifically designed to provide optimal nutrition for pregnant and lactating mothers
  • Omil Mama contributes to 9 calories from Fat in 100 gram of powder and is a cow's milk based low fat milk powder therefore, it provides all the essential vitamins and optimal levels of calcium and iron without supplying excess calories
  • Carbohydrates in Omil Mama are present in the form of Lactose (100%) which is naturally occurring milk sugar
  • Omil Mama is packed with adequate level of bone health supporting calcium
  • Omil Mama provides optimal level of Vitamin A which is necessary for visual health, immune function and fetal growth and development
  • Omil Mama is provided with adequate level of Iron to make normal red blood cells, cell growth of placenta and the developing baby
  • Omil Mama contain optimal levels of Vitamin D and K for calcium balance and bone metabolism

Vinlac Range

To ensure that your little one receive the best quality nutrition, Vinlac provide a complete range of formula products which are uniquely tailored to support optimum growth & development of babies from the early days of life (Vinlac 1 & 2). Whilst for infants with special dietary requirements like in case of lactose intolerance, Vinlac LF offer complete nutrition to support healthy growth of infants with effective management of diarrhea.

Vinlac - 1

Infant Formula for infants from 0 to 6 months of age

Vinlac 1 offers complete nutrition to support healthy growth & development of infants starting from early days of life.

Vinlac 1 provide:

  • Optimal Protein level which is crucial for healthy growth & development of babies
  • 60:40 Whey: Casein ratio close to that found in mature mother's milk (as in terms of protein content, breastmilk is whey-protein dominant)
  • Linoleic Acid & Alpha-Linolenic Acid, essential fatty acids needed for normal growth & development & are also precursors of ARA & DHA to support brain & visual development
  • Iron for formation of hemoglobin in red blood cells & also contributes to normal cognitive development in infants
  • Calcium & Vitamin D to support healthy growth of baby's bones & teeth
  • Vitamin A for maintenance of healthy skin, proper vision & healthy immune system
  • Zinc, Vitamin C & D which play important role in supporting normal function of the immune system

Vinlac - 2

Follow-Up Formula for infants from 6 to 12 months of age

Vinlac 2 is designed to meet the normal nutritional requirements of infants from six months up to 12 months of age, along with the introduction of appropriate complementary foods as part of a mixed weaning diet.

Vinlac 2:

  • Provide infants with the daily requirements of Protein, Vitamins & Minerals to promote healthy growth
  • Supply optimal levels & ratio of essential fatty acids (Linoleic Acid & Alpha-Linolenic Acid) for healthy growth of infants & synthesis of ARA & DHA
  • Is enriched with adequate levels of Carbohydrates to fulfill energy requirements of infants
  • Contain Calcium & Vitamin D to support healthy growth of baby's bones & teeth
  • Is supplemented with Zinc, Vitamin C & D which play important role in supporting normal function of the immune system
  • Contain Vitamin A for maintenance of healthy skin, proper vision & healthy immune system

Vinlac - LF

Lactose Free Infant Formula specially designed for the dietary management of infants who are intolerant to lactose or suffering from the symptoms of diarrhea, bloating, gas & abdominal pain caused by lactose intolerance

Vinlac LF is supplemented with all the essential nutrients required to support healthy growth of infants suffering from Lactose Intolerance.

Easy To Digest — Lactose Free

  • Contain Glucose Syrup Solids as Carbohydrate source which makes the Product easy to digest in lactose intolerant babies

Brain & Visual Development

  • Vinlac LF has been supplemented with balanced ratio of Omega-3 & Omega-6 fatty acids, DHA & ARA to support early brain & visual development in infants

GI Health

  • Contain Nucleotides which are important for normal development, maturation and repair of the GI Tract & may also help to reduce the incidence, duration and severity of diarrhea in infants

With Immune Health Supporting Nutrients

  • Enriched with Zinc, Vitamin C and D which play important role in supporting the normal function of the immune system

With Added Zinc*

  • Contain more Zinc than Standard Infant Formula to help in better diarrheal management (5.4 mg per 100 g of powder)

*More than that found in Vinlac 1 Infant Formula
